Our enhanced Chatwoot deployment now includes an advanced conversation analytics system that provides real-time intelligence about every customer interaction. The new "Analytics & Insights" panel automatically delivers sentiment analysis, intelligent issue categorization, business impact scoring, and escalation risk assessment with a -5 to +5 scale. It also provides conversation quality scoring (0-100%) and customer journey stage detection, all updating in real-time as agents respond to messages.

This enhancement transforms our support from reactive to proactive by enabling data-driven decision making. Support managers can now identify at-risk customers before they escalate, prioritize high-impact conversations that could affect revenue, and monitor agent performance through objective metrics. The system helps prevent customer churn, allows better resource allocation, and provides measurable insights for improving service quality while recognizing valuable customers and expansion opportunities.
