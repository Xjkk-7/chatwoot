# 🚀 Enhanced Chatwoot with Advanced Analytics

This repository contains an enhanced version of Chatwoot with **advanced conversation analytics and AI-powered insights**.

## 🌟 Key Enhancements Added

### **🔍 Smart Conversation Analytics**
- **Real-time sentiment analysis** - Automatically detects positive, negative, neutral conversations
- **Intelligent issue categorization** - Classifies conversations (billing, technical, feature requests, etc.)
- **Business impact scoring** - Identifies high-value customer interactions
- **Escalation risk assessment** - Predicts conversations that may escalate

### **📊 Advanced Analytics Dashboard**
Located in the conversation sidebar as **"Analytics & Insights"**:
- Message count and response time analytics
- Customer journey stage detection
- Conversation quality scoring (0-100%)
- Agent performance metrics
- Risk level monitoring

### **🎯 Business Intelligence Features**
- **Impact Level Assessment** - High/Medium/Low with estimated customer value
- **Quality Scoring** - Excellent (90-100%), Good (70-89%), Needs Improvement (<70%)
- **Risk Management** - Low/Medium/High risk scoring (-5 to +5 scale)
- **Real-time Updates** - Analytics refresh automatically when replying to messages
